Learning Without Letting Go

The complete proposal asks whether inference-before-plasticity produces different static learning, continual behavior and stability–plasticity trade-offs from Backpropagation.

Proposal status: complete and living. The three questions below are canonical. Follow-up experiments refine their answers; they do not replace Q1–Q3.
Q1 · answered for frozen MLP

How do PC and BP differ on the same static MNIST architecture?

PC matches BP once inference is sufficient. T=1 is propagation-deficient; T≥5 produces nearly identical validation curves and final accuracy, at higher compute cost.

  1. BP: 98.267% final test
  2. PC T=5: 98.193%
  3. Learning curves saturate together
Q2 · descriptive answer

How do PC and BP differ under fixed single-pass continual learning?

PC is BP-like on Permuted MNIST. On SplitMNIST and SplitCIFAR-10, lower forgetting accompanies lower adaptation, prequential accuracy and label-aligned acquisition.

  1. Domain shift: near parity
  2. Class incremental: both forget
  3. Causal drift mechanism unresolved
Q3 · finite grid answered

How does PC inference budget change stability, plasticity and cost?

T=5 is the cheapest effective tested default for the two-layer MLP. More relaxation does not monotonically improve behavior, while runtime increases consistently.

  1. T=1: boundary failure
  2. T=5: static saturation
  3. T=10/20: more cost, no general win

Audit boundaries

These constraints travel with every chart and conclusion.

Statistical scope

Three independent seeds support descriptive paired comparisons, not broad superiority or significance claims.

Online scope

Batch size 500/200 is mini-batch continual learning, not strict sample-at-a-time online learning.

Comparator scope

CIFAR “BP” is archived repeated-update RBP. A conventional BP/SGD control is still missing.

Biological scope

Conceptually local errors are implemented with autograd, Adam/SGD, synchronized relaxation and one-hot targets.

Protocol scope

Step 1 updates PC parameters at the last relaxation step; Step 2 updates parameters at all 16 steps.

Project scope

The proposal and Q1–Q3 are complete. A second biologically plausible rule, noise study and bounded-replay study remain outside the implemented scope.
