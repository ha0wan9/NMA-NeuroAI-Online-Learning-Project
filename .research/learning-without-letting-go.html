What did the paper claim on CIFAR-10?

This separates Song et al.'s Figure 4i claim from our reproduction and from our later SplitCIFAR-10 extension.

Yes — narrowly verified

Under Song et al.'s archived, test-informed Figure 4i winning configurations, our exact-as-practical three-seed rerun reproduced all six PC and repeated-update BP best-test accuracies within 1 percentage point and the reported PC advantage (+2.30 pp on average). We did not rerun the seven-learning-rate search or establish general, causal, biological, scaling or continual-learning superiority.

Minimum test error after independent tuning

3 · Comparator detail

“BP” means 16 repeated updates per batch

PC runs 16 inference steps and updates weights at every step. The paper also repeats the BP weight update 16 times per batch for its fairness convention.

  • Useful label: paper-protocol RBP
  • Not conventional one-update BP/SGD
  • Both methods use the same 4.28M-parameter learned architecture
4 · Our verification

Six of six source-target cells passed

We reran the three official seeds with the archived winning learning rate for each method and seed. Every reproduced best accuracy landed within the preregistered ±1 percentage-point source target.

  • Matched initialization and data checksums
  • Finite metrics and valid PC descent
  • 5.75 synchronized update hours

Claim ceiling

What this verification licenses us to say — and what it does not.

  • VerifiedExact-as-practical reproduction of the selected Figure 4i best-test numerical outcome on this hardware/software stack.
  • DescriptivePC's reproduced mean best accuracy is 2.296 pp above paper-protocol RBP; three seeds do not establish a broad significance claim.
  • Not verifiedSuperiority over conventional one-update BP, leakage-free model selection, field-wide scaling, causal reduced interference, or biological superiority.
  • Separate studyReplay-free SplitCIFAR-10 is our project extension. The paper's Figure 4i is static CIFAR-10; its continual-learning panels 4d–e use FashionMNIST.
Protocol details worth remembering
  • Architecture: 3×32×32 → Conv64 → Conv128 → Linear512 → 10, with 4,275,402 learned parameters; PC inserts three latent layers.
  • Data quirk: the archived subset helper omits one example per class, yielding 49,990 train and 9,990 test images.
  • Evaluation quirk: the test loader is shuffled with drop_last=True and is evaluated after every epoch.
  • Selection: each method/seed uses its archived winning learning rate and its best test epoch; our project did not rerun the full seven-rate grid.
  • Update semantics: PC uses T=16 and updates parameters at every inference step; the paper-protocol RBP control also performs 16 updates per batch.
  • Provenance: the local PC library is newer than the archived nested copy, so this is exact-as-practical rather than bitwise reproduction.

Audit boundaries

These constraints travel with every chart and conclusion.

Statistical scope

Three independent seeds support descriptive paired comparisons, not broad superiority or significance claims.

Online scope

Batch size 500/200 is mini-batch continual learning, not strict sample-at-a-time online learning.

Comparator scope

CIFAR “BP” is archived repeated-update RBP. A conventional BP/SGD control is still missing.

Biological scope

Conceptually local errors are implemented with autograd, Adam/SGD, synchronized relaxation and one-hot targets.

Protocol scope

Step 1 updates PC parameters at the last relaxation step; Step 2 updates parameters at all 16 steps.

Project scope

The proposal and Q1–Q3 are complete. A second biologically plausible rule, noise study and bounded-replay study remain outside the implemented scope.
