## Interpretation

The set is intentionally not citation-proportional. It over-samples papers that
connect a mechanism to causal online updates, continual evaluation, or the
associated two-week project. This target-directed bias is disclosed in the
roadmap and prevents large adjacent fields from crowding out the endpoint.

The 2025–2026 papers are visually separated as `frontier` because they are
recent; this label is a caution marker, not a quantified replication claim.
Institution, geography, venue-history and deployment-regime balance remain
explicit audit limitations for a future systematic-review version.
