> Conventions:
>
> - Assign IDs in append order beginning with `P001`; never renumber them.
> - Use the arXiv ID without a version suffix; leave it empty when not applicable.
> - Record `Country` explicitly or as `unknown`; do not infer it from names.
> - Use one primary `Method route`: `predictive-coding`, `spike-local`,
>   `other-local-credit`, `stability-memory`, or `protocol-critique`.
> - `Sub-questions` contains comma-separated SQ IDs from `index.md`.
> - `Dimensions` uses `theory`, `experiment`, `survey`, `critical-review`,
>   and `dataset`.
> - `Stars` follows the canonical four-dimension rubric; star rating is not a
>   superiority claim.
> - `Round` is `R1`, `R2`, and so on; it records when the paper entered.
> - `Status` is `confirmed`, `partial`, or `pending`. Bias audit counts
>   confirmed rows by default.
> - `Repro` remains `pending` until the reproducibility assessment reference is
>   loaded and applied.
> - The full source URL, supported claim and page/table location belong in
>   `claims.jsonl`; unknown metadata is written as `unknown`, never guessed.
