> Conventions:
>
> - This table is the single canonical paper-ID registry. Canonical IDs are assigned in append order and are never renumbered; bare `P###` citations refer to these IDs.
> - `Roadmap source ID` preserves the paper ID from roadmap v1.1 for provenance only. Use old roadmap IDs only in this column and in `paper-id-crosswalk.md`.
> - `SOTA SQs` uses the SOTA namespace `SQ1`–`SQ10` from `index.md`. `Roadmap SQs` uses the distinct roadmap namespace `SQ1`–`SQ8`; matching labels across namespaces are not equivalent and must not be conflated.
> - SOTA coverage calculations ignore rows whose `SOTA SQs` value is `—`; roadmap-only imports were not assessed against the SOTA questions.
> - Use the arXiv ID without a version suffix; use `—` when a roadmap-only import was not assessed for this SOTA field.
> - Record `Country` explicitly or as `unknown`; do not infer it from names.
> - Use one primary SOTA `Method route`: `predictive-coding`, `spike-local`, `other-local-credit`, `stability-memory`, or `protocol-critique`. Roadmap-only imports use `not-assessed-for-sota`.
> - `Dimensions` uses `theory`, `experiment`, `survey`, `critical-review`, and `dataset`; roadmap-only imports use `not-assessed-for-sota`.
> - `Stars` retains the rating from the contributing registry and is not a superiority claim.
> - `Round` records when a paper entered. `roadmap-v1.1-import` marks the 20 roadmap-only additions.
> - `Status` is `confirmed`, `partial`, or `pending`. `Repro` remains `pending` until a reproducibility assessment is loaded and applied.
> - `Roadmap domain`, `Roadmap requirement`, and `Roadmap score` retain roadmap v1.1 annotations. A roadmap score is `relevance/authority/recency/evidence`.
> - `SOTA note` is proposal-specific to the original SOTA survey; roadmap-only imports use `—`. `Roadmap role` is proposal-specific to roadmap v1.1; SOTA-only records use `—`. Neither column is a field-wide finding or an interchangeable assessment.
> - The full supported claim and page/table location belong in `claims.jsonl`; unknown metadata is written as `unknown`, never guessed.
> - P020 metadata is corrected to Marco P. E. Apolinario, TMLR, 2025. The record notes `TMLR 12/2024; OpenReview published 2025-01-06` and links the official OpenReview page.
> - P049 is displayed as 2026 because the publisher records publication on 2026-01-27; its DOI and journal volume carry 2025.
> - P050 is displayed as the 2026 volume/article; the publisher records online publication on 2025-12-26.
