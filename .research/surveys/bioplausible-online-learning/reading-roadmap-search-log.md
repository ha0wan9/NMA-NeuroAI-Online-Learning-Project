# Search Log

Evidence cutoff: **2026-07-15**. This is a target-directed roadmap search, not
an exhaustive systematic review of every paper published before the cutoff.

## Sources and query families

- Existing NeuroAI field map and the associated Neural AI Online Learning
  project bibliography supplied the initial mechanism seeds.
- Publisher and proceedings pages were preferred for the selected records:
  Nature/Nature Communications/Nature Machine Intelligence/Nature
  Neuroscience, Frontiers, eLife, PMLR, NeurIPS/OpenReview, CVF, PubMed and DOI
  landing pages.
- Query families combined `biologically plausible`, `online`, `continual`,
  `streaming`, `local learning`, `predictive coding`, `eligibility trace`,
  `spiking`, `BTSP`, `temporal credit`, `dendritic` and `loss of plasticity`.
- Backward/forward lineage checks centered on P011 (predictive coding and BP),
  P018 (e-prop), P037 (BTSP), P044 (incremental-learning protocols) and P007
  (loss of plasticity).

## Evidence policy

- Mechanism summaries use publisher/proceedings abstracts or selected
  full-text passages where available.
- Metadata-only evidence is used only for historical placement or problem
  naming, not for detailed mechanism claims.
- Cross-paper route choices are labeled curator recommendations and are tied to
  the associated two-week project's constraints.
- The 2025–2026 nodes are marked `frontier`; this label indicates recency and
  does not assert a quantified replication level.

## Known limits

- Institution and country distributions were not extracted or quantified.
- Public code repositories were not audited for environment setup, issue
  status, implementation parity, or independent reproduction.
- Searches were performed primarily in English and may underrepresent adjacent
  terminology or non-English literature.
- Publication-year labels follow the publisher's issue/article convention for
  P049/P050, with online-publication notes retained in the shared canonical
  [`paper_index.md`](paper_index.md) and shared [`claims.jsonl`](claims.jsonl).
