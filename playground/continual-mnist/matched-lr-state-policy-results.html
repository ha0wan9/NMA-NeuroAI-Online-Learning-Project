What happens when optimizer memory resets?

We kept the learned network intact, cleared only the optimizer's internal state at each task boundary, and compared how Adam and CLASSP responded under Backpropagation and Predictive Coding.

01 · Question

Weights are knowledge. Optimizer state is the notebook.

The intervention asked whether carrying or clearing that notebook changes continual learning—and whether the answer depends on the learning method and optimizer.

w
Network parameters

Always preserved

The MLP's learned weights and biases remained across all 20 tasks. Reset never returned the network to its initialization.

+
s
Parameter-optimizer state

Persistent or cleared

Adam carries gradient moments. CLASSP carries its accumulator. Task-reset emptied this state before tasks 1–19.

Analogy, not mechanism: the notebook metaphor explains what was manipulated. It does not prove how PC or CLASSP works biologically.

05 · Meaning

The central pattern is a plasticity–stability tradeoff.

CLASSP reset made the network adapt faster in both learning methods. PC's distinctive result appeared in the smaller retention penalty, not in a uniquely larger plasticity boost.

Established observation

Plasticity returned

Reset increased CLASSP online accuracy and adaptation under both BP and PC, while increasing forgetting in both.

Interpretation

PC buffered the cost

PC may preserve prior-task performance better during the reset-induced plasticity burst, allowing a small positive final balance.

Counterclaim

Transient scale may explain it

Reset enlarged first updates, and Adam was exceptionally sensitive. The interaction need not imply a special PC×CLASSP mechanism.

Bridge versus confirmation: open seed 42 showed a −2.19-point PC–CLASSP effect, while all five held-out seeds were slightly positive. The relative RPC and Ω directions were positive in both. This is why the inspected seed was excluded.
06 · Limits

What the experiment supports—and where the claim stops.

Passing a preregistered directional gate is evidence for the frozen configuration. It is not a license to generalize beyond the data, intervention, architecture, or analysis.

Supported here

  • RPC and Ω were positive in all five held-out seeds.
  • PC–CLASSP reset had a small positive final effect in all five.
  • Reset exposed a repeatable plasticity–retention tradeoff.
  • All frozen state, stream, source, order, and pairing checks passed.

Not established

  • Statistical significance or a population-level effect.
  • A causal PC×CLASSP mechanism or biological synergy.
  • Reset as a generally superior continual-learning strategy.
  • Generality beyond this fixed-LR Permuted-MNIST MLP.
What follow-up would best test the mechanism?

A new preregistered protocol should match the first-update magnitude after task boundaries across reset and persistent conditions, perhaps through a learning-rate ramp or state-rescaling intervention. If Ω remained positive after transient scale was matched, the optimizer-transient counterclaim would weaken.

Why not call 5/5 “statistically significant”?

The protocol registered a directional sign gate, not an inferential test with a population model and calibrated error rate. Five deterministic seeds show consistency within this experiment, but the claim ceiling explicitly excludes statistical significance.

Did reset prevent catastrophic forgetting?

No. Forgetting increased in every configuration. PC–CLASSP had the smallest retention penalty among the PC reset comparisons and enough adaptation benefit to end slightly ahead on final average accuracy.
